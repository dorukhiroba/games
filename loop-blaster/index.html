How to play

  1. Tap a blaster at the front of a lane to send it onto the belt.
  2. As it rides around the board it fires at the first block it sees in each row and column, but only blocks of its own color.
  3. If it finishes a lap with ammo left, it parks in a bay. Tap it to send it round again.
  4. Clear every block to win. If a blaster comes back and every bay is full, the level fails.
