How to play

Set every gem back in its place

The ring shows which colour belongs there. A small round gem is sitting in the wrong spot.
Lift a gem only if one side is open: the picture's edge, an empty spot, or a gem already home. Dim gems are boxed in.
A lifted gem jumps home if a matching spot is open. If not, it waits in storage and drops in the moment one opens.
+1
Fill storage with nowhere to go and you're out of room. Each finished colour adds a slot. Pictures you finish without boosters earn three stars.
