How to play

Everyone starts with a Town Center, three villagers and a scout. Villagers gather and build; soldiers fight. Destroy every enemy building and unit to win. Players on the same team share vision and win together.

Select
Click a unit, or drag a box. Double-click selects all of that type on screen. Shift adds to the selection.
Command
Right-click ground to move, a tree, berry bush or gold mine to gather, a foundation to build, an enemy to attack. On touch, tap a target while your units are selected.
Build
Select villagers, pick a building in the panel, then click to place it. Hold Shift to place several.
Counters
Spearmen beat Horsemen, Horsemen beat Archers, Archers beat Spearmen.
Camera
Arrow keys, screen edges, middle-drag or the minimap. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
Hotkeys
QWERASDF panel buttons · H Town Center · . idle villager · , army · Ctrl+1–9 groups · Del delete · Enter chat
